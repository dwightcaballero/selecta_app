EXPENSE vs END OF DAY SHORTAGE - IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
====================================================

GOAL
----
1. Salesman expenses and Dealer expenses (Cash) must reduce the cash the
   salesman is expected to hand over, so the End of Day "Shortage / Overpaid"
   shows the REAL difference.
2. When creating or editing a Salesman expense or Dealer (Cash) expense, a
   checkbox decides if the expense is deducted from that day's End of Day cash.
   This is for forgotten expenses from earlier days, which must NOT change the
   shortage.

WHY THE PROBLEM HAPPENS NOW (root cause)
----------------------------------------
- services/endofday_services.dart already computes
  expectedcashonhand = cash sales - (salesman + dealer cash expenses), live.
- BUT the "Shortage / Overpaid" number shown on the End of Day page is the
  STORED breakdown.discrepancy. It is only recalculated while the cash count is
  being edited/saved in breakdown_page.dart, using the expected amount at that
  time.
- So when an expense is added AFTER the cash count was saved, Expected goes
  down but Shortage stays the same.
- Side issues found:
  a) ExpensesService.getExpensesForEndOfDay() has catch (e) { return []; }.
     A read error silently makes all expenses disappear.
  b) Its end time is 23:59:59, so an expense at 23:59:59.5 is skipped.
  c) Verifying a day only sets the verified flags. It does not save the final
     numbers, so a locked day can still change if expenses change later.

DECISIONS (confirmed)
---------------------
1. Shortage is calculated LIVE until the day is verified by the dealer. After
   verification, a frozen copy of the numbers is shown.
2. Checkbox label: "Deduct from End of Day cash".
   Default ON when the expense date is today, OFF when it is a past date.
3. An expense with the checkbox OFF keeps its real date (reports and Profit &
   Loss stay accurate). It is simply not part of any day's cash count.
4. The checkbox shows only for Salesman expenses and Dealer expenses paid by
   Cash. Online dealer expenses never affect cash, so no checkbox.
5. Old expenses (no field saved) are treated as ON (deducted), so past figures
   do not change.
6. Salesman can set the checkbox when creating a Salesman expense. Only the
   dealer can change it on an existing expense. Every change is audit-logged
   (existing Helperfunctions.logCreate / logUpdate).
7. No extra "Cash paid on" date for now. (See "Future option" at the end.)
8. A salesman's back-dated expense (expense date earlier than today) needs
   DEALER APPROVAL (Phase 5B). Until approved it is not deducted.
9. When the checkbox is OFF, a reason is required (Phase 5A).

WHICH CASE USES WHICH SETTING (for the help text and training)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Situation                                         Setting
------------------------------------------------  -----------------------------
Expense happened today, cash taken from today's    Date = today, ON
  cash on hand
Forgotten expense from an earlier day that is      Date = that day, ON
  NOT yet verified                                 (that day's shortage shrinks)
Forgotten expense from an earlier day that is      OFF (today is not affected)
  already verified/locked
Forgotten expense, but the money is paid back      Date = today, ON
  today out of today's cash on hand
Paid by owner's own money or bank transfer         OFF

PHASE 3 - END OF DAY CALCULATION
--------------------------------
Files: lib/services/expenses_services.dart, lib/services/endofday_services.dart,
       lib/dto/endofday_dto.dart, lib/controllers/endofday_controller.dart,
       lib/models/breakdown.dart
3.1 expenses_services.dart: getExpensesForEndOfDay()
    - Use a half-open range: expenseDate >= startOfDay AND < startOfNextDay.
    - Stop swallowing errors (remove catch-all return []). Let the caller show
      an error message instead of wrong numbers.
3.2 endofday_dto.dart: add
      double excludedExpenseAmount   // cash expenses with checkbox OFF
    Keep salesmanExpenseAmount, dealerExpenseCashAmount,
    dealerExpenseOnlineAmount. expenseAmount = only expenses where
    affectsEndOfDayCash is true.
3.3 endofday_services.dart: in the expenses loop
      - salesman: if affectsEndOfDayCash -> salesmanExp += amount, else
        excluded += amount
        (pending and rejected expenses go to excluded; also count them in a
        new pendingApprovalCount and pendingApprovalAmount on the DTO)
      - dealer cash: same logic with dealerCashExp
      - dealer online: dealerOnlineExp (unchanged, never deducted)
    expenseAmount = salesmanExp + dealerCashExp
    expectedcashonhand = cashamount - expenseAmount
3.4 endofday_controller.dart: ONE shared function for shortage:
      discrepancy = (actual cash counted + bank deposit) - expectedcashonhand
      (rounded to 2 decimals, same as recompute() today)
    - recompute() keeps using it.
    - fetchEndOfDayData(): when the breakdown is NOT verified, recompute
      breakdown.discrepancy and endOfDayData.discrepancy from the live
      expected amount and the saved counted cash + bank deposit. Do not rely on
      the stored discrepancy.
    - When the breakdown IS verified, use the frozen values (3.5).
3.5 Freeze on verify:
    - models/breakdown.dart: add optional fields (default 0 / null, backward
      compatible): finalExpectedAmount, finalDiscrepancy,
      finalExpenseDeducted, finalExpenseExcluded.
    - verifyAndSettleBreakdown(): before saving the verified record, fetch the
      live End of Day data, store those final values, and save. Show the frozen
      values for verified days.
    - Old verified records without frozen values: fall back to stored
      expectedAmount / discrepancy (current behaviour).
3.6 Keep breakdown.expectedAmount and breakdown.discrepancy updated in the
    database when the live value differs (so exports and other readers see the
    right number): update them when the End of Day / Breakdown page loads and
    the day is not verified.

PHASE 5B - DEALER APPROVAL FOR BACK-DATED SALESMAN EXPENSES
-----------------------------------------------------------
Files: expenses.dart, expenses_services.dart, expenses_controller.dart,
       expenses_page.dart, expenselist_page.dart, endofday_controller.dart,
       breakdown_page.dart, firestore.rules
5B.1 Which expenses need approval:
       - Created by a salesman AND expense date is earlier than today
         -> approvalStatus = 'pending'.
       - Everything else (dealer-created, or salesman expense dated today)
         -> 'approved' automatically, so daily work has no extra step.
     This applies whether the checkbox is ON or OFF. ON matters most: a
     back-dated expense with ON lowers that day's expected cash, which is the
     way a shortage could be covered up.
5B.2 While 'pending' or 'rejected', the expense is NOT deducted (see
     affectsEndOfDayCash). The shortage stays visible until the dealer decides.
5B.3 Dealer actions (expense list "Pending approval" filter with a count badge;
     also a button on the expense detail screen):
       - Approve: sets 'approved', approvedBy, approvedDate. It now counts
         according to its checkbox.
       - Reject: sets 'rejected' with a required reason. Dealer may also edit
         or delete it.
     Salesmen see the status (Pending / Approved / Rejected + reason) on their
     expense, but cannot change it.
5B.4 Closed-day rule applies here too: the dealer cannot approve an expense
     that would change a verified day (Phase 5). Show the same message; the
     dealer must unverify the day first.
5B.5 A day cannot be verified while it still has pending-approval expenses
     dated on that day. Add to verifyAndSettleBreakdown() and
     validateBeforeBreakdown() with the message: "There are still N expense(s)
     waiting for approval on this date." (Same style as pending deliveries.)
5B.6 Editing: if a salesman-created expense is edited by the dealer, it stays
     as is; approval is not reset. If the amount, date or checkbox of an
     APPROVED salesman expense is changed to a back date by anyone, set it to
     'pending' again unless the dealer made the change.
5B.7 Audit log: every approve / reject / status change goes through
     Helperfunctions.logUpdate (who, when, reason).
5B.8 firestore.rules: only dealers can write approvalStatus, approvedBy,
     approvedDate and rejectReason, so the rule is enforced on the server and
     not only in the app. Salesmen creating a record can only create with
     'pending' (back-dated) or 'approved' (today). Confirm the exact rule
     syntax against the existing role checks in firestore.rules.

RISKS / NOTES
-------------
Risk 1 - Checkbox left ON/OFF wrongly, so a day looks "over" or "short"
  - If the cash really came out of today's cash on hand, the expense must be
    dated today and ON. If it is left OFF, today looks "over" by that amount.
  - Controls: the required reason dropdown (5A.1), the help text (Phase 2), the
    closed-day message (Phase 5), and the dealer-only hint that points to a
    not-deducted expense matching an overpaid amount (5A.3).

Risk 2 - An expense used to cover a real shortage
  - The real danger is a made-up or inflated back-dated expense that is ON,
    because it lowers that day's expected cash and hides the shortage.
    (An OFF expense never hides a shortage; at worst it makes a day look
    "over".)
  - Controls: dealer approval for back-dated salesman expenses, and they are
    not deducted until approved (5B); a day cannot be verified with pending
    approvals (5B.5); salesman cannot use OFF on a today-dated expense (5A.2);
    reasons are required (5A.1); every change is audit-logged; the dealer-only
    "Not deducted" review list shows patterns (5A.4).

REVIEW ANSWERS (confirmed)
--------------------------
Q1. Dealer approval: YES for back-dated salesman expenses (Phase 5B). This
    replaces the earlier "no approval" answer. Dealer-created expenses and

FUTURE OPTION - "CASH PAID ON" DATE (not included now)
------------------------------------------------------
Add a separate "Cash paid on" date to an expense. The expense date would drive
reports and Profit & Loss, and the "Cash paid on" date would decide which day's
cash count it reduces.
Recommendation: do NOT add it now.
  - With the checkbox, reason, approval and closed-day rules, every case is
    already handled by choosing the right expense date: if the cash left on
    day B, date the expense day B.
  - The only thing lost is that such an expense appears in Profit & Loss on day
    B instead of day A. Salesman expenses are not in Profit & Loss at all, so
    this only matters for dealer expenses that cross a month end.
  - It is cheap to add later: one optional field that defaults to the expense
    date, so old records and reports do not change.
Revisit it if, after a month or two of use, the dealer review list (5A.4) shows
many expenses with the reason "Paid on an earlier day" that the dealer says
were actually paid back from a later day's cash, or if dealer expenses often
cross a month end and the monthly Profit & Loss looks wrong.
