1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document outlines the end-to-end design and implementation plan for the 
Salesman Attendance System in Selecta Ops.

Based on operational review and customer confirmations:
1. Morning Clock-In Gate (Optimistic Auto-Unlock):
   - When a salesman launches the app for the first time each day, they are 
     prompted to clock in.
   - Captures GPS coordinates and verifies location against the dealer's depot 
     geofence.
   - Once clocked in, the record enters "Pending Confirmation", but the app 
     immediately unlocks (Optimistic Access) so early-morning van loading, 
     picklist verification, and delivery operations are never stalled by dealer 
     transit/sleep delays.
2. Dealer Clock-In / Clock-Out Confirmation:
   - Dealers receive instant push notifications and have a dedicated 
     one-tap approval sheet (with "Approve All" and individual actions).
3. End-of-Day (EOD) Cash Breakdown Gate:
   - For single-vehicle operations, all clocked-in salesmen on duty for the day 
     must clock out before anyone can view or edit the Cash Breakdown.
   - Guarantees no salesman leaves without clocking out and ensures complete 
     crew accountability before final money reconciliation.
4. Payroll Integration (Weekly Timesheet in Payslip Generator):
   - Dealer views daily clock-in, clock-out, total hours, and day credits for the 
     selected week directly inside PayslipGeneratorPage.
   - Uses standard day credit calculation:
       * >= 6.0 hours worked = 1.0 day credit
       * 4.0 to < 6.0 hours worked = 0.5 day credit (half-day)
       * < 4.0 hours worked = 0.0 day credit
   - Dealer retains full manual override capability for days worked and credits.

2. DATA ARCHITECTURE & FIRESTORE SCHEMA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Firestore Collection: `attendances`
   Document ID Format: `att_{YYYY-MM-DD}_{salesmanUsername}`
   Ensures exactly one attendance document per salesman per operational day.

   Fields:
   - id: String (e.g., 'att_2026-10-31_juan_dc')
   - dealerName: String (e.g., 'Selecta North Hub')
   - salesmanUsername: String (e.g., 'juan_dc')
   - salesmanFullName: String (e.g., 'Juan Dela Cruz')
   - date: Timestamp (start of day normalized)
   - dateString: String (e.g., '2026-10-31')
   
   Clock-In Details:
   - clockInTime: Timestamp?
   - clockInLatitude: double?
   - clockInLongitude: double?
   - clockInDistanceToDepot: double? (meters)
   - clockInWithinGeofence: bool
   - clockInStatus: String ('pending' | 'approved' | 'rejected')
   - clockInApprovedBy: String?
   - clockInApprovedAt: Timestamp?

   Clock-Out Details:
   - clockOutTime: Timestamp?
   - clockOutLatitude: double?
   - clockOutLongitude: double?
   - clockOutDistanceToDepot: double? (meters)
   - clockOutWithinGeofence: bool
   - clockOutStatus: String ('pending' | 'approved' | 'rejected')
   - clockOutApprovedBy: String?
   - clockOutApprovedAt: Timestamp?

   Summary & Payroll Metrics:
   - totalHours: double (e.g., 9.75)
   - dayCredit: double (calculated: 1.0, 0.5, or 0.0)
   - dealerOverrideCredit: double? (if dealer manually edited day credit)
   - overrideRemarks: String?
   - isCompleted: bool (true once clocked out)
   - createdAt: Timestamp
   - updatedAt: Timestamp

B. Configuration Update (`configurations` collection):
   Depot Geofencing Settings:
   - depotLatitude: double? (e.g., 14.5995)
   - depotLongitude: double? (e.g., 120.9842)
   - depotRadiusMeters: double (default: 300.0 meters)
   - depotAddress: String (human-readable warehouse address)
   - attendanceEnabled: bool (default: true)

3. DETAILED WORKFLOW & UX DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WORKFLOW 1: MORNING CLOCK-IN GATE (SALESMAN)
1. Salesman opens the app.
2. `SalesmanDashboardPage` verifies if an attendance document exists for 
   `today` under `att_{today}_{username}`.
3. If not clocked in:
   - A non-dismissible `ClockInDialog` or `ClockInGateSheet` is displayed.
   - Shows current time, live GPS acquisition status, and depot distance indicator:
       * Green badge: "At Depot (Within 50m)"
       * Orange badge: "Off-Depot (420m away - will be logged for dealer review)"
   - Salesman taps "Clock In":
       * Fetches high-accuracy GPS coordinates via `Geolocator`.
       * Creates/updates `attendances` doc with status `pending`.
       * Sends FCM push notification to Dealer:
         "Salesman Juan Dela Cruz clocked in at 06:45 AM (At Depot)."
       * Closes sheet and immediately unlocks the dashboard.
4. Top status chip on Salesman Dashboard:
   - Displays: "🟢 Clocked In: 06:45 AM (Pending Approval)"
   - Salesman can tap this chip at any time to view their day's timecard.

WORKFLOW 2: END-OF-DAY (EOD) CASH BREAKDOWN LOCK
1. Salesman finishes deliveries and navigates to `EndofdayPage`.
2. `EndOfDayController.validateBeforeBreakdown()` checks attendance status:
   - Queries `attendances` for `dateString == selectedDate` and `dealerName`.
   - Checks if any salesman who clocked in has NOT yet clocked out.
3. If any clocked-in salesman has not clocked out:
   - The "View/Edit Cash Breakdown" button is disabled or intercepted.
   - Shows blocking warning dialog / bottom banner:
     "⚠️ Cash Breakdown Locked: All salesmen must clock out before cash breakdown 
      can be opened.
      Pending Clock-Out:
      • Juan Dela Cruz (Active since 06:45 AM)
      • Pedro Santos (Active since 07:05 AM)"
   - Includes a direct "Clock Out Now" button for the logged-in salesman.
4. Salesman taps "Clock Out":
   - Captures GPS location and depot distance.
   - Computes `totalHours` and standard `dayCredit`.
   - Updates document with `clockOutTime` and `isCompleted = true`.
   - Sends notification to Dealer.
5. Once all salesmen on duty are clocked out:
   - "Cash Breakdown" immediately unlocks and can be edited/settled as normal.

WORKFLOW 3: DEALER REVIEW & CONFIRMATIONS
1. Dealer Dashboard has an "Attendance Status" widget / appbar badge:
   - E.g., "Attendance: 2 Clock-Ins Pending Approval".
2. Tapping opens `AttendanceManagementSheet`:
   - Lists today's salesmen with:
       * Clock-in time & depot proximity badge (e.g., "At Depot" vs "3.2 km away").
       * Clock-out time & total duration.
       * Status chip (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
   - "Approve All" action button for rapid 1-tap morning approval.
   - Individual "Approve" and "Reject" buttons.
3. When dealer approves:
   - Document updated with `clockInApprovedBy = dealerUsername`, `clockInStatus = 'approved'`.
   - Salesman's chip turns solid green: "🟢 Clocked In: 06:45 AM (Approved)".

WORKFLOW 4: PAYROLL / TIMESHEET IN PAYSLIP GENERATOR
1. In `PayslipGeneratorPage`, dealer selects a week and salesman.
2. The page loads the 7-day attendance records for that week.
3. Above the "Days Worked" field, an interactive `WeeklyTimesheetCard` is rendered:
   - Lists Monday to Sunday:
       * Day & Date
       * Clock-In & Clock-Out times
       * Total Hours
       * Calculated Day Credit (1.0, 0.5, or 0.0)
       * Approval Status chip
   - Shows "Auto-Calculate Days" button:
       * Sums the credits (e.g., 5 days @ 1.0 + 1 day @ 0.5 = 5.5 days).
       * Populates `_daysWorkedController.text = '5.5'`.
   - Dealer can tap any day row to manually adjust the credit or add notes
     (e.g., granting full day credit for a 5-hour breakdown or excused duty).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. RISK MITIGATION & EDGE CASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GPS Disabled or Permission Denied:
   - If GPS permission is denied or location is temporarily unavailable, allow 
     clock-in with an "Off-GPS / Location Unavailable" flag rather than blocking 
     operations. Flagged for dealer review.
2. Historical Dates in End-of-Day:
   - When viewing an old past date in EOD, do not block cash breakdown if 
     the date was before the attendance feature launch date (grandfathering).
3. Dealer Absent or Inactive:
   - Because of Optimistic Auto-Unlock, salesman operations are never halted 
     if the dealer is unavailable to approve immediately.
4. Single-Vehicle Teammates:
   - In EOD page, the crew list clearly shows who hasn't clocked out so they 
     can quickly remind each other or clock out together before counting cash.
